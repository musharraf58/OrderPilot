# Payment Policy (Hearth & Desk Co., a fictional demo store)

## Accepted payment methods

We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard and American Express). Payments are processed securely by our payment provider. We do not accept cash, cheques or bank transfers.

## When you are charged

Placing an order does not charge your card. The order stays "pending" until you complete payment for it, and its status then changes to "paid". Pending orders are not prepared or shipped.

## Prices and currency

All prices are in US dollars. Sales tax, where it applies, is calculated and shown at checkout before you pay.

## Payment security

Card details are handled by our payment provider. Hearth & Desk Co. never sees or stores your full card number.

## Payment problems

If your payment is declined, check your card details or try another card; your bank can tell you why a payment was declined. If you were charged twice for the same order, contact our support team at support@hearthanddesk.example and we will refund the duplicate charge.
