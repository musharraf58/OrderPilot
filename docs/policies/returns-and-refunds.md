# Returns and Refunds Policy (Hearth & Desk Co., a fictional demo store)

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Gift cards
- Items that were personalised or engraved for you
- Opened food items, such as spice refills

## How to start a return

Returns are handled by our support team. Email support@hearthanddesk.example with your order date and the items you want to return, and the team will send you return instructions and a return label.

The OrderPilot chat assistant cannot start a return or issue a refund. It can only show you your orders and explain this policy.

## Return shipping costs

If an item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item, we pay for return shipping. For any other return, the return shipping cost of $6.95 is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we refund the item price to your original payment method within 5 business days. Your bank may take a further 3 to 10 business days to show the refund. Original shipping costs are not refunded unless the item was damaged or wrong.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact support within 7 days of delivery and include a photo. We will send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping.
