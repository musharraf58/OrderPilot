# Order Cancellation Policy (Hearth & Desk Co., a fictional demo store)

## Which orders can be cancelled

Only orders with the status "pending" can be cancelled. A pending order has been placed but not yet paid, so nothing has been charged and nothing has been prepared.

## How to cancel a pending order

You can cancel a pending order yourself in your account, or ask the OrderPilot chat assistant. The assistant always shows you which order it is about to cancel and asks you to confirm in the app before anything changes. Typing "yes" in the chat is not enough.

## Paid orders

Paid orders cannot be cancelled in your account or through the chat assistant. If you need to stop a paid order that has not shipped yet, contact our support team at support@hearthanddesk.example as soon as possible. Support will try to stop it before it is packed, but this cannot be guaranteed.

## Shipped orders

Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled. Once the order has been delivered, you can return eligible items under our Returns and Refunds Policy.

## After cancelling

A cancelled order cannot be reopened. If you change your mind, please place a new order.
