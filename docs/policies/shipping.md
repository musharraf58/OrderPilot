# Shipping Policy

*Hearth & Desk Co. is a fictional store used to demo OrderPilot. This is not a real company's policy.*

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States only. We do not ship to P.O. boxes or internationally.

## When your order is prepared

We start preparing an order once it has been paid. Orders that are still pending (not yet paid) are not prepared or shipped. Paid orders are packed within 1 to 2 business days.

## Shipping options and delivery times

- **Standard shipping:** 3 to 7 business days after the order ships. Costs $5.99, and is free for orders over $75.
- **Express shipping:** 1 to 3 business days after the order ships. Costs $14.99.

Delivery times are estimates and are counted in business days (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays).

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, its status changes to "shipped" and we email you a tracking link. You can also see the status of every order in your account.

## Late or lost parcels

If your order has not arrived 10 business days after it shipped, contact our support team at support@hearthanddesk.example and we will trace the parcel with the carrier.
